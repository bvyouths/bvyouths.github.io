01Who we are

A network of
passionate leaders.

With a focus on Youth Advocacy, Cohesion and Empowerment, we aim to create and nurture a network of passionate leaders committed to giving back to society and driving meaningful change.

Our committee is made up of dedicated 15–35 year old youths with a shared vision of fostering positive social impact, inspiring community engagement and driving initiatives that promote growth and inclusivity.
