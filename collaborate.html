02Join us

Join the
network.

Youth (Singapore Citizen/PR aged 15–35) who are interested in serving the community in a greater capacity are welcome to join us!

Role 01

Committee Member

  • Attend monthly meetings and co-organise grassroots events and programmes.
  • Participate in at least 2 meetings and 2 events before we consider your application to join as a committee member.
Role 02

Community Volunteer

  • Volunteer at our events and programmes on an ad-hoc basis, at your convenience.
